ACSM 12th Edition — Source & Access Policy
==========================================

Source: ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription, 12th Edition (2025)
Editors: Cemal Ozemek, Amanda Bonikowske, Jeffrey Christle, Paul Gallo
Publisher: Wolters Kluwer (Lippincott Williams & Wilkins)
ISBN: 978-1-9752-1920-8
Pages: 1,153 / 12 chapters + appendices

PDF location (NOT in repo):
  /mnt/documents/acsm-12e.pdf  (agent-accessible only; outside repo; never bundled)

IP rule (binds every round):
  - NEVER render verbatim chapter prose to end users (trainer or client UIs, PDFs, exports).
  - ALLOWED: paraphrased rules, numeric thresholds, FITT ranges, normative tables as
    derived parameters, citation strings of the form "ACSM 12e §X.Y" or "ACSM 12e Tbl X.Y".
  - Storage: paraphrased prose lives only in `acsm_chapters` / `acsm_sections` (RLS deny-all,
    service-role only). Derived facts in `acsm_recommendations` / `acsm_contraindications` /
    `acsm_normatives` are trainer-readable.
  - No public RAG chat over the book. Internal retrieval feeds prompts; outputs are
    paraphrased + cited.

Edition handover (per Round-1 brief):
  - Wholesale migrate to 12e values where they differ from current code (which is 11e-leaning).
  - Where 12e is LESS conservative than the existing threshold, flag in the gap report and
    require explicit approval before adopting.
