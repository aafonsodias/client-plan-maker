Role in the knowledge stack:
  - ACSM 12e   = WHAT to prescribe (FITT-VP, contraindications, populations — clinical).
  - Bompa 6e   = HOW to plan over time (periodization, biomotor abilities).
  - NSCA 3e    = HOW to teach, select, progress, modify (applied programming, cuing,
                 technique, populations from a practical lens).

IP rule (mirrors ACSM 12e and Bompa 6e policy):
  - NEVER render verbatim prose to end users (trainer or client UIs, PDFs, exports).
  - ALLOWED when ingestion happens: paraphrased rules, exercise selection heuristics,
    technique cues, population modifications as derived structures, citation strings
    of the form "NSCA Essentials 3e §X.Y" or "NSCA Essentials 3e Tbl X.Y".
  - Storage when ingested: paraphrased chapter notes go to a server-only table
    (RLS deny-all, service-role only). Only derived facts (exercise recommendations,
    cue libraries, progression rules, population modifications) become trainer-readable.

Cross-source schema (Model B — approved):
  When ingestion happens, NSCA shares the same source-agnostic tables as Bompa and
  ACSM where the domains overlap:
    - periodization_phases / _sequences / _microcycle_patterns
