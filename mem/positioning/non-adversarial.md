---
name: Non-adversarial positioning
description: Sell Protocol by what the trainer gains, never by attacking Excel/ChatGPT/Trainerize/RP/generic apps
type: preference
---
# Rule
Never sell Protocol by diminishing another tool. No competitor names in user-facing landing copy. No "vs", "melhor que", "sem viver no Excel", "guarda dados", "apps genéricas". Tone: calm, premium, self-contained, confident. Let the user connect the dots.

# Forbidden visible phrasings
- "sem viver no Excel" / "without living in Excel"
- "melhor que ChatGPT" / "more controllable than ChatGPT"
- "Excel guarda dados, Protocol liga"
- "apps genéricas fazem X, Protocol faz Y"
- "Protocol vs <tool>" anywhere on landing
- Any matrix-style competitor table

# Preferred PT
- "Da avaliação ao plano, com lógica visível."
- "Avaliação estruturada, protocolo editável, adaptação baseada no log."
- "Menos dispersão. Mais continuidade entre avaliação, plano e execução."
- "Organize clientes, sessões, planos e progresso num só fluxo."
- "O treinador mantém o controlo: revê, ajusta e aprova."
- "Cada plano nasce de dados concretos: objetivo, disponibilidade, equipamento, limitações e histórico."

# Preferred EN
- "From assessment to plan, with visible logic."
- "Structured assessment, editable protocol, log-based adaptation."
- "Less fragmentation. More continuity between assessment, plan, and execution."
- "Organize clients, sessions, plans, and progress in one workflow."
- "The coach stays in control: review, adjust, and approve."
- "Each plan starts from concrete inputs: goal, availability, equipment, limitations, and history."

# Comparison reframing
Replace any "Protocol vs X" matrix with "Connect what usually stays separate" / "Ligue o que normalmente fica separado" — 6 chips: Assessment, Plan, Sessions, Logbook, Adaptation, Progress.

# Scope
Internal component names (e.g. AntiChatGPTSection) are fine if not visible. Audit/positioning docs are allowed to name competitors when explaining what to avoid.
