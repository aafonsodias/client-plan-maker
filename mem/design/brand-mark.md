FORGE mark = simple geometric hammer striking a clean cubic block from above, with three small amber sparks (#D4A24C / #E8A547) at the impact point. Hammer + block in deep graphite (monochrome). Sparks are the ONLY chromatic element. Modern SaaS / scientific instrument aesthetic — never anvil, never medieval, never cartoonish. Used via <Logo /> (raw) and <BrandMark /> (chrome plate with amber under-glow). Source: src/assets/forge-logo.png.
