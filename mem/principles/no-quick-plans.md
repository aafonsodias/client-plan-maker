---
name: No "quick plans"
description: We don't sell shortcuts. The product is golden-standard ACSM-grade assessment scaled to available equipment. Default = no-equipment baseline; depth unlocks as equipment is declared.
type: constraint
---
We **never** ship UI, copy, or features framed as "quick plan", "fast plan", "plano rápido", "5-click plan", or any variant that implies skipping assessment.

The product's contract with the trainer is the opposite: we do **the most evidence-based assessment possible with the equipment available**, following ACSM and best global practice. The default is the simplest no-equipment baseline (chair stand, sit-and-reach, 6MWT, RPE-anchored capacity tests, etc.) — but it is still a real assessment, not a shortcut. As the trainer/client declares more equipment (DXA, force plate, dynamometer, GPS, HR strap, RM rack), the assessment surface and the resulting plan depth grow.

**Why:** "Quick plan" is the value proposition of every ChatGPT wrapper and Trainerize template. Our edge is the opposite — defensibility per session. Calling our flow "quick" undercuts the only moat we have.

**How to apply:**
- Never add `/plans/quick`-style entry points to dashboard, AppShell, or onboarding.
- Plan creation is always preceded by (or coincident with) assessment intake. The brief editor's defaults come from intake answers, not from "skip and generate".
- Copy reframes: instead of "Gerar plano rápido", say "Avaliar e prescrever" / "Assess & prescribe" or "Começar avaliação".
- New assessment fields should be gated by an equipment flag on the trainer/client profile, so trainers without DXA never see DXA-derived prescriptions.
