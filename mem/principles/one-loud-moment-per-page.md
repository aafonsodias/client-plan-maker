---
name: One loud moment per page
description: Every page reserves exactly one bold gesture; the rest stays quiet
type: preference
---
Every authenticated page reserves exactly one "loud moment" (amber under-glow, large numeral, or 600ms reveal). All other surfaces stay editorial-quiet. See mem://design/aesthetic-direction.md for the per-page table. Why: prevents the inner app from drifting into generic SaaS noise. How to apply: when adding a new section/card, check the page's reserved loud moment and keep the new element quiet.
