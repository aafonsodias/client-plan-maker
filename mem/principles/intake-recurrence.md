Intake link = trainer asks client for a fresh assessment snapshot when needed.
- Hide / collapse the panel once the link has been opened OR submitted.
- Surface as "Pedir nova avaliação" action when trainer wants new data.
- Continuous signals (sleep, RHR, HRV, steps, wearable sync) flow through the client dashboard, NOT through the intake link.
**How to apply:** never leave a "Not opened yet" state visible after the link has actually been opened. Realtime subscribe to clients row.
