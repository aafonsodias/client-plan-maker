DEFERRED — do not build until explicitly scheduled.

# What
A simple client-facing education surface (page or downloadable ebook) explaining how to support gym results outside of training itself.

# Topics
- Sleep
- Nutrition basics
- Protein intake
- Hydration
- Alcohol
- Steps / daily movement
- Pain expectations
- Soreness vs warning signs
- What to do if pain appears
- What NOT to do when symptoms worsen
- Recovery habits
- Adherence expectations
- Lifestyle factors that improve or limit progress

# Tone (non-negotiable)
- Educational, not authoritarian.
- Clear consequences, not moral judgment.
- Evidence-aware, not fear-based.
- Frame: "you are free to choose, but here is what tends to support or undermine results."

# Why deferred
Not in MVP critical path. Belongs to retention/expansion phase, after the PT operating loop (clients → packs → schedule → sessions → revenue) is solid. Building it earlier risks scope creep and dilutes the "weekly operating system for serious PTs" positioning.
