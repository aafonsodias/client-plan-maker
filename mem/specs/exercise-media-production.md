# Exercise media — production notes

Companion to [media quality taxonomy](mem://specs/exercise-media-quality.md). These are the rules for filming a clip that can later be tagged `reference_demo` or `verified_technique`.

## Hardware

- Phone on tripod, lens height = subject's hip for ground-based lifts, sternum for standing lifts.
- Two angles per exercise: **frontal** (square to subject) and **lateral** (90° from movement plane).
- 1080p minimum, 60fps preferred for fast lifts (snatch, jerk, jump variants).

## Framing

- Whole body in frame for the entire rep, including bar path top-to-bottom.
- Neutral background (matte wall, no logos, no other people).
- Clothing contrasts with floor and background (no black on black).
- No music. Ambient room audio only — coach voice may be added in post.

## Length & content

- 6–12 seconds per clip. Two clean reps, no setup walk-in, no rack-out.
- Slate at frame 1: exercise canonical key + date (YYYY-MM-DD) + version (e.g. `back_squat_low_bar · 2026-05-01 · v1`).
- One technique per clip. Variants (high-bar vs low-bar, conventional vs sumo) get separate clips.

## Subject

- Demonstrator must be competent at the movement. A poorly executed `reference_demo` is worse than no clip — it teaches the wrong shape.
- Founder demos are tagged `founder_demo`, not `reference_demo`, until reviewed.

## Post

- No filters, no slow-mo on the master clip (a slow-mo derivative may live alongside).
- Optional `stickfigure_overlay` derivative is a separate asset, never replaces the real clip.

## Out of scope

No media table, no upload UI, no CDN strategy, no review workflow ships from this doc. Filming standard only.
